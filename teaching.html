Teaching

I teach machine learning, signal processing, programming and digital electronics to undergraduate engineers at the Islamic University of Technology (IUT), and I publish my course materials openly.

Materials are on GitHub, and recorded lectures for several courses are on my YouTube channel.

Courses

AI and ML

Artificial intelligence and machine learning

The machine learning workflow in Python, from exploring and cleaning data to scikit-learn pipelines, validation, hyperparameter tuning and imbalanced data, and then deep learning with CNNs and transfer learning in TensorFlow. Taught as a lecture course with a lab.

GitHub

Deep learning for computer vision

CNN design and its use in vision tasks: regularization, data augmentation, pre-trained networks and transfer learning, with medical imaging case studies such as skin cancer and malaria parasite detection.

GitHub

Signal processing

Biomedical signal processing, and signals and systems

Generating, filtering and analyzing signals in MATLAB, including biomedical signals such as EEG and ECG, together with digital image processing, segmentation and registration for biomedical imaging. Taught with labs.

Signal processing Image processing Biomedical imaging

Programming

Python

Python fundamentals for engineers and data scientists: data structures, functions, and the NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib and Seaborn libraries.

GitHub

MATLAB: a problem-solving approach

An 11-week course on writing efficient MATLAB code through problems: arrays and data structures, control flow, functions, vectorization, data handling, plotting, algorithms, GUIs and Simulink, with weekly assignments, labs and practice quizzes.

GitHub

Power systems

Power systems

Generation, transmission and distribution of electric power: transmission line parameters, load flow, fault analysis, and system stability and protection.

Digital electronics

Digital logic design

Number systems, Boolean algebra, combinational and sequential circuits, flip-flops, counters and registers, with hands-on lab work.

Other

Electrical and electronic workshop, control system engineering lab, numerical methods lab and semiconductor physics.
